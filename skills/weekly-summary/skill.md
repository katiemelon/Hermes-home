# Weekly Summary

An end-of-week review and forward-look to keep your household on track.

## What it does
- Summarises what happened this week (events, tasks completed, anything that slipped)
- Flags what's coming up next week that needs attention
- Prompts you to plan anything that's drifting

## Getting started
Tell Hermes when you'd like your weekly summary — Sunday evening is popular:
> "Send me a weekly summary every Sunday at 6pm"

## Using it
> "Give me my weekly summary"
> "What do I need to action before Monday?"
> "What fell through the cracks this week?"
