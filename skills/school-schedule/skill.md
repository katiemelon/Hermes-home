# School Schedule

Keeps track of term dates, school events, and pickup/dropoff coordination.

## What it does
- Reminds you of upcoming school events, excursions, and dress-up days
- Tracks term dates and school holidays
- Coordinates pickup arrangements and reminds the right people

## Getting started
Share your school's term calendar during setup — you can paste it as text or forward the email. Hermes will extract the key dates and remember them.

## Using it
> "What's on at school this week?"
> "Remind me about the school fete on Friday morning"
> "Who's picking up the kids today?"
