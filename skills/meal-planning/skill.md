# Meal Planning

Weekly meal suggestions and grocery list generation tailored to your family.

## What it does
- Suggests a week of dinners based on your family's preferences and dietary needs
- Generates a grocery list from the plan
- Remembers what your family likes and avoids over time

## Getting started
Tell Hermes your family's basics during the bootstrap interview:
- Number of people and ages
- Dietary requirements or strong dislikes
- How much cooking time you realistically have on weeknights

## Using it
> "Plan dinners for next week"
> "We're doing a big cook on Sunday, what should I make?"
> "Add the ingredients for tonight's dinner to my shopping list"
