# Email Drafting

Drafts emails on your behalf — you review before anything is sent.

## What it does
- Writes emails in your voice based on a quick brief
- Handles school communications, replies, follow-ups, and requests
- Always shows you the draft before sending — nothing goes out without your approval

## Using it
> "Draft an email to the school about Friday's excursion permission"
> "Reply to the party invitation and say we can make it"
> "Write a follow-up to the plumber who hasn't replied in two weeks"

## Note on Gmail integration
Requires Gmail to be enabled in `mcp.json`. Without it, Hermes will write the draft and you copy-paste it yourself.
