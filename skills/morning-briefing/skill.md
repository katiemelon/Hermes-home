# Morning Briefing

Sends you a proactive summary each morning before your day starts.

## What it includes
- Today's weather
- Your calendar events for the day
- Any pending tasks or reminders
- Anything time-sensitive that needs attention

## Configuration
Set your preferred briefing time during the bootstrap interview, or tell Hermes:
> "Send me a morning briefing every day at 7am"

## Customising
Tell Hermes what you want included or excluded:
> "Add the kids' school schedule to my morning briefing"
> "Skip the weather, I don't need it"
