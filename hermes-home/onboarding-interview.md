# onboarding-interview.md

This is the AI's question bank for the household setup interview.

**Do not ask all of these at once.** Ask in batches of 2–4. The goal is a natural conversation, not a form. Skip questions where the answer is already clear from context.

---

## Batch 1 — Household basics

Ask these first. They inform almost everything else.

1. What's your first name, and how would you like Hermes to address you?
2. How many people are in your household? (Adults, kids — rough ages are fine.)
3. What's your biggest daily friction point at home right now — the thing that consistently falls through the cracks or takes up mental energy?

---

## Batch 2 — Rhythm & schedule

Ask these second, once you know the household shape.

4. Roughly what time does your household morning start? (When do people leave for school/work?)
5. Do you have a consistent weekly rhythm — like school pickup at the same time each day, or a regular weekly shop?
6. Are there any recurring deadlines or events that regularly sneak up on you? (e.g. school permission slips, bill payments, birthday gifts)

---

## Batch 3 — Module-specific questions

Ask only the questions relevant to modules the user seems interested in, based on Batch 1 & 2.

### Morning Briefing
7. What would you want in a morning briefing? (Weather, calendar for the day, tasks, news, something else?)
8. Where do you want to receive it — Telegram message, email, or just available if you ask?

### Meal Planning & Groceries
9. Do you meal plan at all currently, or is it more ad hoc?
10. How many meals a week would you want help planning? (Some families just want dinners; others want the full week.)
11. Are there any dietary needs, strong preferences, or foods anyone won't eat?
12. Where do you usually shop? (This helps with grocery list formatting.)

### Kids' School Schedule
13. How many kids, and what ages/school stages are they at?
14. Do you currently have a way of tracking school events and term dates, or is it mostly in your head?
15. What kinds of school reminders would actually be useful? (e.g. "tomorrow is non-uniform day", "book fair starts Monday")

### Email Drafting & Sending
16. What kinds of emails do you regularly dread writing or procrastinate on?
17. Are there specific people or organisations you email often? (School, GP, work colleagues?)
18. Do you want Hermes to be able to send emails on your behalf, or just draft them for you to review?

### Weekly Summary
19. What would a useful end-of-week review look like for you? (Tasks completed, upcoming week preview, something else?)
20. What day/time would you want it delivered?

### Cron Automations
21. Are there things you wish happened automatically at a set time — reminders, reports, check-ins?
22. Any tasks you do every week that feel repetitive and robotic?

---

## Batch 4 — Tone & access

Ask these near the end.

23. How do you want Hermes to talk to you? (Warm and chatty? Short and efficient? Somewhere in between?)
24. Which platforms do you want to reach Hermes on? (Telegram is the easiest — do you have it installed?)
25. Is there anything about your household or family that Hermes should always keep private — never mention to others, never include in outputs?

---

## Notes for the AI

- If someone mentions a child's name during the interview, note it but do not repeat it back unnecessarily. Treat children's names and details as sensitive.
- If the user says "I don't know" or "whatever you think", make a reasonable recommendation and briefly explain why.
- Aim to cover Batches 1–2 and the most relevant Batch 3 questions. Batch 4 is always required.
- Total interview should feel like a 5–10 minute conversation, not a long intake form.
